# Survivor's Mark — Design Reference

**Status:** LOCKED — decided 2026-05-20

---

## What It Is

The Survivor's Mark is the fourth pillar of Guild Score and the most personal. It is the
accumulated legacy identity of your dynasty — shaped by quest choices each guild master
makes during their run, curated at prestige, and passed forward in evolved form to the
next generation.

It is hidden from the player until the WorldFamous world awareness tier unlocks. Until
then it contributes silently to Guild Score. The reveal is intentional — the player
discovers both what their dynasty has become and that it was building all along.

Four templates define the mark space. Each template is a category of story-flavored
legacy trait earned through specific quest choices.

## How It Forms During a Run

Certain quests present meaningful choices. Each choice nudges the guild master's personal
mark toward one or more trait templates. Choices feel in-world and specific — not abstract
sliders or alignment axes. The player may not know the mark is building in early play.

---

## Prestige Curation

At prestige, the departing guild master's mark is shown as a blend of traits developed
during their run. The player chooses which traits to carry forward to the next generation.

- Early prestiges: fewer carry-forward slots
- Later prestiges: more slots as dynasty power grows
- Traits not carried forward fade — that guild master's specific character doesn't fully
  survive, which is narratively honest

The incoming guild master inherits the curated blend and builds their own layer on top.
The mark evolves across generations — never fully reset, never fully frozen.

---

## The Hidden Reveal

At WorldFamous world awareness tier, the player unlocks the ability to see:
1. Their dynasty's Survivor's Mark as a composed identity — what all the generations
   built together
2. How it has been contributing to Guild Score all along

This is the intended design payoff: the discovery that something significant was building
silently is the reward for reaching WorldFamous tier.

---

## In the Demon King Fight

The dynasty's accumulated Survivor's Mark is a factor in the demon king fight. The founding
hero survived the original battle — that survival is the seed of the mark. Every guild
master's choices layered on top of it become the dynasty's unique advantage in the final
confrontation. No other guild can bring the same mark to that fight.

---

## Connection to the Founding Hero

The founding guild master's survival of the original demon king catastrophe is not
explained at game start. It is revealed through play — via quest choices that develop
the mark and, at WorldFamous tier, the full Survivor's Mark quest line. The mark's origin
is that moment of survival.

---

## Relationships and the Mark

Guild master relationships — with high-tier adventurers, with allied guilds, with dynasty
offshoots from previous prestiges — are formed through co-op quests and shared history.
These relationships can contribute to mark development and to the curation options
available at prestige. Full relationship mechanics are deferred design.

---

## Deferred

- Exact carry-forward slot count per prestige tier (balance tuning)
- Visual representation of the mark (UI work)
- Full relationship mechanic (deferred design)
- Magic Rewind as a potential mechanic tied to quest choices (possible future, not designed)
