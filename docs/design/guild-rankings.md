# Guild Rankings — Design Reference

**Status:** LOCKED — decided 2026-05-20

---

## What It Is

The Guild Rankings is a recurring seasonal competition — every 91 in-game days (one
season) — where guilds in your region compete across four trial types. Your results
determine your Guild Rank, one of the four pillars of Guild Score.

This is the game's primary engagement heartbeat. The Conclave (every 5 in-game years)
is the milestone event. The Guild Rankings is the regular rhythm that pulls players back.

---

## The Four Trial Types

You choose which adventurers to send to each trial. Picking the right adventurers for
the right trials is the core active decision — strategic deployment, not real-time
reaction. Different trial types reward different adventurer strengths, so the squad
composition decision matters.

## Guild Rank

Competition results move your rank up or down against other guilds in your region.
Guild Rank is visible and competitive — other guilds you know by name are on the board.

Guild Rank is one of the four Guild Score pillars:

| Pillar | Source |
|---|---|
| Guild Rank | Guild Rankings competition results |
| Reputation | World awareness tier, quest history, relationships |
| Dynasty Power | Accumulated prestige strength, adventurer tiers, buildings |
| Survivor's Mark | Hidden until WorldFamous; story-flavored legacy traits |

---

## Relationships via Competition

Competing against the same guilds season after season builds history. Consistently
beating a rival creates a grudge. Being overtaken and clawing back builds a rivalry
arc. This is one source of guild master relationships — alongside co-op quests and
dynasty offshoots (guilds born from your previous prestiges).

---

## Coexistence with the Conclave

These two events serve different purposes and must not be merged.

| Event | Cadence | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Guild Rankings | Every season (91 in-game days) | Competitive rank, regular engagement heartbeat |
| Conclave | Every 5 in-game years | Dynasty scoring, Skill Borrow reset, major decisions |
