# Demon King Endgame — Design Reference

**Status:** LOCKED — decided 2026-05-20

---

## World Context

Before the game starts, a demon king nearly wiped out every guild in the world. The
founding hero survived. Almost no one else did. The founding hero knows what the demon
king is capable of and knows the demon king will return. Every prestige, every generation
trained, every building built — it is all preparation for that return.

The demon king endgame is the destination the whole dynasty is building toward. It is
real, reachable, and meaningful. See also: docs/design/survivor-mark.md for how the
founding hero's survival seeds the Survivor's Mark.

---

## Unlocking the Fight — Option B into C

Two gates must both clear before the player can challenge the demon king.

### Gate 1 — The Quest Chain (Option B)

A late-game quest chain unlocks around WorldFamous world awareness tier. These quests
recover lost knowledge from the original demon king battle: what went wrong, what the
demon king's weaknesses are, what it takes to win. The founding hero's personal
survival is threaded through this chain.

The backstory is delivered through play, not a text dump. Completing the chain is
required to unlock the fight and provides specific tactical advantages (demon knowledge
items) used during it.

### Gate 2 — Dynasty Power Threshold (Option C)

Specific dynasty strength requirements must be met: world awareness tier, adventurer
tier requirements, accumulated prestige count. Exact values are balance tuning —
not yet locked.

Once both gates clear, the option to challenge the demon king appears. The player
chooses when to trigger it.

## The Fight — Multi-Front Campaign

The demon king fight is a campaign, not a duel. The guild master does not fight
personally. The dynasty does — and everything built across every prestige shows up
on that battlefield.

Three fronts run simultaneously:

### The Army
Your guild's overall combat depth vs the demonic vanguard. Resolved by: adventurer
count and tier spread, building strength, allied guild contributions. Combat guild
type provides a significant advantage here. This is the baseline — without sufficient
depth the other fronts are unreachable.

### The Champions
Named demon champions vs your best adventurers. The core chess-piece decisions live
here. You assign specific adventurers to specific champions. Quest chain knowledge
reveals each champion's weakness — deploy the right adventurer and you have an edge,
deploy wrong and they take damage (injury, not permanent loss). Demon knowledge items
from the quest chain can counter specific champion abilities.

### The Source
The demon king's power has a root. Attacking it requires ancient knowledge from the
quest chain plus the guild master's class abilities. Knowledge guild type provides a
significant advantage here. An Archmage approaches the source differently than a
Diplomat who built a coalition. The guild master's class and the dynasty's accumulated
Survivor's Mark both factor here.

## Active Gameplay — Chess Piece Deployment

The fight is strategic, not real-time. You see the battlefield state, assign your
pieces, and watch it resolve. Between phases you respond to outcomes and redeploy.

Your pieces:
- Named adventurers from your high-tier roster
- Guild master class ability (class-dependent contribution)
- Allied guilds (relationships built across the dynasty)
- Ancient knowledge / demon knowledge items (from quest chain)

The demon king responds with abilities between phases. Quest chain preparation lets you
counter specific abilities. Without it you absorb the cost.

---

## Outcome

**Win:** The world changes. The demon king is defeated. Your dynasty is the most famous
guild in history. A post-demon-king content layer unlocks — new threats, world
rebuilding, new story. The fight is a milestone into a new phase of play, not a
true ending.

**Lose (underprepared or poor deployment):** You survive but retreat. The demon king
is not defeated. The dynasty takes real consequences — weakened state, setback event.
You regroup and try again when ready. No permanent run wipe.

---

## Post-Demon-King Era

Content design is deferred until the fight itself is built. Principles:
- The world is permanently changed — the dynasty's history becomes world history
- New threats or challenges emerge in the changed world
- Dynasty continues and can still prestige in the new era
- The Survivor's Mark takes on new meaning in a world the demon king no longer rules
